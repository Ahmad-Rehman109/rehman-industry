# Rehman Industry — 10 Logo Prompts (10 different themes)

Tool: Ideogram. Aspect 1:1. Style "Design" (flat/vector) for 1–8, "Realistic" for 9.
Magic Prompt: On. Generate, then send me the ones you like.

--- logo-1 (Molten Flow Monogram) ---
A modern logo of the letter R formed from a single continuous stream of glowing molten plastic, smooth liquid ribbon with an orange-to-amber gradient and soft inner glow, set on a deep charcoal background, fluid and dynamic, clean vector emblem, premium industrial brand mark. No text, no extra elements.

--- logo-2 (Steel Mould Parting Line) ---
A minimalist logo emblem of two interlocking steel mould halves meeting at a thin glowing seam, the negative space between them forming an abstract letter R, brushed graphite grey with a molten-orange parting line, flat geometric vector, precise and premium. No text.

--- logo-3 (Pellet to Part) ---
A clean logo showing small plastic resin pellets rising and fusing into one solid formed shape, dotted granules flowing upward into a smooth amber droplet-like part, gradient from scattered to whole, white background, modern flat vector mark. No text.

--- logo-4 (Hexagonal Engineering Badge) ---
A bold hexagonal industrial badge logo containing a stylized injection-moulding screw and nozzle motif, gunmetal steel with amber accents, strong geometric engineering emblem, crisp lines, premium and technical. No text.

--- logo-5 (Negative-Space Precision R) ---
A high-contrast logo of a bold geometric letter R cut out of a solid rounded-square tile, as if ejected from a mould, matte black tile with warm metallic copper edges and a subtle inner bevel, minimal Pentagram-style vector mark. No text.

--- logo-6 (Injection Gate / Cavity) ---
An abstract logo of a single droplet of molten polymer entering a circular mould cavity through a precise gate, concentric precision rings radiating outward, deep teal and steel-silver palette, clean modern vector symbol, calm and technical. No text.

--- logo-7 (Blueprint Cross-Section) ---
A fine technical line-art logo resembling an engineering blueprint cross-section of an injection mould forming a part, thin precise white lines and measurement ticks on a deep navy background with cyan accents, premium drafting aesthetic, vector. No text.

--- logo-8 (Heritage Monogram Crest) ---
A refined industrial monogram crest combining the letters R and I, slim gold linework on a deep charcoal shield, subtle gear-tooth and mould motif, timeless premium emblem, balanced and elegant, vector. Small "EST. 1985" allowed at the bottom.

--- logo-9 (3D Moulded Symbol) ---
A sleek three-dimensional abstract brand symbol that looks like a glossy precision injection-moulded plastic part, smooth curved geometry with a soft highlight, photorealistic studio render on a clean dark gradient background, premium and tactile. No text.

--- logo-10 (Bold Industrial Monogram) ---
A confident chunky geometric letter R with a clean notch cut and two small ejector-pin dots suggesting a moulded part, single bold industrial-red colour on a white background, ultra-minimal modern logo, strong and memorable, flat vector. No text.
