## STAGE 1 — Google Search Console (get indexed) · ~20 min, do today

This tells Google your site exists and to crawl it.

1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with your Google account → "Add property".
3. Choose the **Domain** option → type `rehmanindustry.com` → Continue.
4. Google shows a **TXT record**. Copy it.
5. In **Cloudflare → your domain → DNS → Records → Add record**:
   - Type: `TXT`
   - Name: `@`
   - Content: paste the value Google gave you
   - Save.
6. Back in Search Console → click **Verify** (wait a minute if it fails, retry).
7. Once verified → left menu → **Sitemaps** → enter `sitemap.xml` → Submit.
8. Left menu → **URL Inspection** → paste `https://rehmanindustry.com` →
   "Request indexing". Repeat for `/services`, `/portfolio`, `/contact`.

✅ Done when: sitemap shows "Success" and pages start appearing under "Pages".

6. Phone: **+92 300 9642762**  ·  Website: **https://rehmanindustry.com**
7. Hours: Mon–Sat 9:00–18:00.

## STAGE 3 — Reviews (drives local rank + trust) · ongoing, start now

1. In your Google Business Profile dashboard → "Ask for reviews" → copy your
   short review link.
2. Send it (WhatsApp is fine) to your real customers with a one-line ask:
   "Agar humara kaam acha laga to please ek Google review chhor dein: [link]"
3. Target: **5 reviews in week 1, 10+ within a month.** Keep them coming slowly
   and steadily (a burst of 20 in one day looks fake to Google).
4. Reply to every review (even a thank-you). Google rewards active profiles.

✅ Done when: you have 5+ genuine reviews with replies.

## STAGE 5 — Backlinks (domain trust) · ongoing

Google trusts sites that other credible sites link to.

1. Ask 2–3 customers/suppliers to link to rehmanindustry.com from their sites.
2. Link from your own Facebook + LinkedIn pages.
3. Join any trade body / chamber of commerce that has an online member list.
4. Quality over quantity — a few real links beat dozens of spammy ones.

✅ Done when: you have 3–5 real links pointing to the domain.
